Privacy Policy

AnggaTextPad

AnggaTextPad is a simple and lightweight note-taking application designed to help users create and organize personal notes quickly, efficiently, and securely.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. All data — such as notes and categories — is stored locally on the user's device.

2. Internet Connection

This application works entirely offline. No internet connection is required to use any of its main features, ensuring user privacy and data security.

3. Advertising and Tracking

  • No advertisements are displayed within the application.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools are integrated.
  • No data is transmitted to any external servers.

4. Data Access

All notes created in AnggaTextPad are only accessible on the device where the application is installed. We do not have access to any user data or content.
